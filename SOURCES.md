# Sources — Friday 27 November 2026

Every story drawn in `entry.png` was reported on Friday 27 November
2026 and cross-checked in at least two independent outlets before it
was used. Items that looked fresh but turned out to be older were
dropped; they are listed at the end so the checking is auditable.

## Lead story

**Trump claims "victory at hand" in the war on Iran; Iran denies the
Kuwait drone attack.**
Al Jazeera's 27 Nov live coverage: President Trump, in Thanksgiving
calls to US troops, claimed the US has "victory at hand" in its war on
Iran and offered Tehran the chance to negotiate a deal. Dawn's 27 Nov
live coverage carried the same claim and the same Kuwait incident:
two Italian nationals were killed by falling debris during the
interception of Iranian drones over Kuwait and two more were injured.
Iran's foreign minister Abbas Araghchi denied responsibility, called
the incident "suspicious" and a "pre-cooked recipe for war", and
expressed condolences to his Italian and Kuwaiti counterparts (both
liveblogs). Reuters reported the war's market fallout the same day.

## Grid stories

1. **New Zealand — final count leaves the coalition one seat short.**
   Reuters (27 Nov): the final election count, released Friday, gives
   Prime Minister Christopher Luxon's centre-right coalition 65 of
   130 seats — National 40, ACT 13, NZ First 12 — one short of a
   majority; Labour holds 36. The centrist Opportunity Party (7 seats,
   leader Qiulae Wong) holds the balance; Wong said she would
   negotiate first with the largest party. Also reported by Nikkei
   Asia, RNZ, CNA and the Straits Times.

2. **Australia — sanctions on two men over IRGC-linked arson attacks.**
   Reuters (27 Nov, 03:17 GMT): Australia placed counterterrorism
   financing sanctions on Zohair Hassib and Kazem Hamad, saying the
   pair used a "complex chain of proxies" to orchestrate the 2024
   antisemitic arson attacks on a Sydney kosher restaurant and
   Melbourne's Adass Israel Synagogue on behalf of Iran's Islamic
   Revolutionary Guard Corps; Foreign Minister Penny Wong announced
   the move. Also reported by the Jerusalem Post, the Straits Times
   and the Times of Israel.

3. **Japan — ¥56.3 trillion defence plan under consideration.**
   Reuters (27 Nov), citing Asahi reporting of government sources:
   Japan is considering defence spending of ¥56.3 trillion
   (about $353 billion) over the five years from fiscal 2027, below
   what defence officials sought; Tokyo plans to brief the US.
   Finance Minister Satsuki Katayama said Friday that spending should
   follow defence needs rather than a preset numerical target. Also
   carried by AsiaOne.

4. **EU — budget talks called off without a deal.**
   AFP via the Business Times (27 Nov): EU leaders called off a
   second day of budget talks on Friday after more than 12 hours of
   Thursday talks failed to produce consensus on the 2028–34 budget
   (near €2 trillion) and a new $49 billion loan for Ukraine, per
   Chancellor Merz; President Macron set a year-end deadline and
   leaders meet again on 17–18 December. AP reporting (via
   Moneycontrol and the Manila Times) and Al Jazeera carried the same
   adjournment.

5. **Oil and markets — Brent heads for its first weekly gain in four.**
   Reuters (27 Nov): Brent crude stood at about $96.19 a barrel,
   heading for its first weekly gain in four weeks, as attacks on
   tankers in the Strait of Hormuz and on Saudi energy facilities
   threatened supply; the dollar headed for a third straight monthly
   gain. Dawn and Al Jazeera reported the same market picture.

6. **US — Thanksgiving storm aftermath.**
   Reported on Friday 27 Nov (UTC) by USA Today and the Wall Street
   Journal: more than one million US power outages as of 7:30 p.m. ET
   Thursday (00:30 UTC Friday), 1,207 flight cancellations by 8 p.m.
   ET (FlightAware; 2,295 delays), and New York's Macy's Thanksgiving
   parade moved to Saturday 28 November. The figures were first
   published on 27 Nov; the storm itself struck on Thanksgiving Day,
   which is how the panel words it.
